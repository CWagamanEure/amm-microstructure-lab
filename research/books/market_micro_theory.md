# MMT - OHara Notes

- Easier for informed traders to hide larger trades in market structures that have larger discrete batched trading intervals, as opposed to more continuous markets where trades are immediately reflected in the price.
- This does not neccesarily matter for AMMs because, although blocks are mined at discrete intervals, AMM trades are reflected continuously in the price because they are instantaniously released in to the mempool for inspection and possibility of arbitrage. 
- naturally, the above two points require the assumption that there is only 1, monopolistic informed trader, as that one informed trader would attempt to milk the signal by attempting to prevent his discovery by the market maker. 
- In the instance that there are multiple informed traders, with the same signal, they would attempt to trade in a mannor that recognizes the other informed traders in the market, and attempt to make the most profit off the signal.
- Naturally, as the number of informed traders in the market approaches infinity, the efficiency of the market also approaches infinity, where informed traders are competing to make profit off their signal.
- This brings up the question as to what is the point where the informed traders no longer enter the market, or are able to sustain their signal mining processes when the extractable profit is so negligable in such a highly efficient market.
    - Clearly, informed traders in highly efficient markets are weeded out to the ones that are able to extract the signal for the cheapest cost and outpace the other informed traders to the signal extraction.
